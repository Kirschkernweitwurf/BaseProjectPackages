# Close Menu Button

🔖 **Component** · for everyone

Closes a menu when the button is clicked. Your typical "Back" or "X" button.

## Where to put it

On a GameObject that has a **Button** component.

## Fields

| Field | Required | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Menu To Close | Yes | The menu identifier asset of the menu you want to close. |

## Good to know

- If the menu is not open, clicking does nothing.
- The menu to close does not have to be the menu the button sits in, but that is the usual case.
